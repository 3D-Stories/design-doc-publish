Runbook: carrier certificate failover

doan action to performchecka condition to confirm before moving on

How to fail Halyard over to a secondary carrier certificate. Follow this under pressure. Do not improvise.

Read step 1 before you touch anything

Failing over with a stale secondary is worse than staying degraded. Step 1 is the check that stops that, and it takes eleven seconds.

  1. 1Verify the secondary is validhalyard cert check --slot secondary
  2. 2Confirm the primary is the faulthalyard probe carrier-a --tls-only
  3. 3Announce in the incident channelState which carrier and which slot.
  4. 4Swap the slothalyard cert promote --slot secondary
  5. 5Watch the miss ratehalyard watch miss-rate --window 60s
  6. 6Stand down or escalateUnder 1% within five minutes, or page the carrier liaison.

What the swap actually does

Failover requested
Load the secondary certificate
Does it chain to a trusted root?
yes
Promote it to the active slot
no
Refuse and keep the primary
Drain connections using the old certificate
Miss rate recovers

If step 4 refuses

It refuses for exactly one reason: the secondary does not chain. That is not a bug and it is not worth debugging mid-incident. Stay on the primary, accept the degradation, and page the carrier liaison — they can re-trust our existing certificate faster than we can mint a new one.
